Privacy Policy for Noor — Islamic Dawah

Last updated: May 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Noor — Islamic Dawah application ("Noor", "the app", "we") handles your information.

Summary

Noor is a fully offline application. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app does not require an account, does not connect to any backend server, and does not include any analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies.

Information We Collect

We collect no personal information of any kind. This includes:

  • No name, email, phone number, or contact details
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers
  • No usage analytics or behavioral data
  • No crash reports or diagnostic data sent to our servers

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The app saves the following information only on your device using Android's local storage (AsyncStorage):

  • Cards you have saved to your collection
  • Your preferred theme (light or dark mode)

This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to us or any third party. You can clear this data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's storage in your device settings.

Internet Access

Noor does not require an internet connection to function. All Islamic content (Quranic verses, hadiths, duas, and stories) is bundled inside the app at the time of installation.

Third-Party Services

Noor does not integrate with any third-party advertising networks, analytics platforms, social media services, or external APIs.

The app uses Expo Updates to deliver app improvements over the air. This service may receive your device's IP address and basic device information solely for the purpose of delivering updates. Expo's privacy practices are described at https://expo.dev/privacy.

Children's Privacy

Noor is suitable for users of all ages. Because the app does not collect any personal information, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.

Data Sharing

We share no data with third parties because we collect no data.

Your Rights

Since no personal data is collected, there is no data to access, modify, or delete from our systems. You retain full control over locally stored content (saved cards, theme preference) on your own device.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date. Significant changes will be communicated through an in-app notice when reasonably possible.
